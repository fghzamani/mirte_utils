What to say

    Using it at the stand

    Space play or pause · ← → previous or next scene · F fullscreen · C clean view. It loops, so it can run unattended next to the robot.

    To make a video, go fullscreen and screen-record one loop (OBS, or GNOME's recorder with Ctrl+Alt+Shift+R).

    The warehouse scenes run a small built-in simulation. The robot's readings come from the drawn map, and its choices follow the tuner's rules: does it fit, can it stop in time, is the risk under the cap, is an arm switch worth it. The setting values are illustrative. The results scene uses the paper's simulation results.

    Robot dimensions are placeholders near the top of the script (R and POSES). Replace them with the real MIRTE Master numbers.
